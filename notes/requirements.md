## 3. Pilot and roadmap

Six concise fields, in the brief's order:

1. **Risultato e perimetro**: useful output, users, exclusions.
2. **Dati e dipendenze**: inputs, access, checks before starting.
3. **Lavoro necessario**: usable capabilities, checks, additional development.
4. **Valore e qualità**: proposed metric, baseline to gather, representative examples.
5. **Responsabilità**: DS, FDE, client roles.
6. **Controlli e revisione**: human control, main error, change/stop condition.

- [ ] Plan discovery to pilot across the first six weeks, user verification and possible extension.
- [ ] Every milestone: observable result, owner and condition to proceed.
- [ ] Treat six weeks as conditional planning, not a delivery guarantee.
- [ ] Explain trade-offs and what would change the decision; justify scope cuts/delays.

Source: 01 p.2. Proposed verification criteria suffice; a full test plan is not requested.
